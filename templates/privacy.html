Deepak's CRM may process information such as:

Information processed through Deepak's CRM may be used for:

  • Managing customer contacts

WhatsApp message information may be processed through Meta's services in accordance with Meta's applicable terms and policies.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the CRM against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the CRM service, maintain records, comply with applicable requirements, and resolve disputes.

7. User Rights

Users may request correction or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Deepak's CRM is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

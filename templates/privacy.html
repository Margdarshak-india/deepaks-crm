Privacy Policy

Information about data handled by Margdarshak's CRM.

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Margdarshak's CRM respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information handled through the CRM and WhatsApp messaging services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp messages
  • Message delivery and status information
  • Campaign information
  • Information voluntarily provided by users

2. How We Use Information

  • Managing customer contacts
  • Sending authorized WhatsApp communications
  • Managing messaging campaigns
  • Recording message delivery status
  • Receiving and managing incoming WhatsApp messages
  • Maintaining CRM records
  • Improving reliability and functionality

3. WhatsApp Data

Margdarshak's CRM uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to send and receive WhatsApp messages. WhatsApp message information may be processed through Meta's services under applicable terms and policies.

4. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the CRM, including infrastructure and communication services.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide the CRM service, maintain records, comply with requirements, and resolve disputes.

7. User Rights

Users may request correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The CRM is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time with a revised Last Updated date.
